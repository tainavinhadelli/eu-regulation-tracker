    What this is

    An independent reference for tracking EU sustainability rules that are new, being phased in or being changed. It is modelled on the independent GHG Standards Tracker but covers EU product, packaging, green-claims, supply-chain and reporting law instead of US rules. It is not legal advice – always check the linked primary source before acting on a date.

    How the data is maintained

    Each week the sources below are checked for new developments. New items go into the Updates feed, and a regulation's status, milestones and confidence are adjusted if something changed. Once a month every entry is re-checked against the primary legal text. Entries marked Not yet verified were added from general knowledge and still need that check.

    How to read the confidence ratings

    Each regulation's rating answers one question: how likely is it that what is described here is what companies will have to comply with?

    Confirmed Final published text or formally adopted decision.

    High Adopted or officially planned, but a date is conditional or a procedural step remains.

    Medium Outcome genuinely open – negotiations, consultations or court cases could change it.

    Low Early signal only, or the file is dormant.

    How to update the dashboard

    All content sits in one data block at the top of this page's file (id="data"). To add a development, copy an existing item in updates, paste it at the top, change the text and date, and set meta.last_updated. The recency groups, countdowns and timeline recalculate automatically.

    Scope

    In scope: EU regulations and directives on reporting, carbon pricing, energy efficiency, products, packaging, batteries, repair, waste and producer responsibility, chemicals, deforestation, forced labour, supply-chain due diligence and green claims that are new or changing, plus Austrian transposition where relevant and the Austrian Waste Management Act (AWG 2002). Also tracked: the voluntary standards that sustainability reporting depends on – the GHG Protocol and ISO 14001 – with other ISO standards listed for reference in the Regulation guide. Out of scope: non-EU rules.

    For voluntary standards the stages read differently: drafting, public consultation, final draft, published, transition period and required use.
